Privacy

SarkariRadar is a free quiz that matches you to Indian central government schemes. It is built and run by Aditya Pratap. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

Your quiz answers stay in your browser

The quiz runs entirely in your browser on your device. When the page loads, it downloads the list of schemes from this site, and your answers are matched against that list on the device itself. Your answers are not sent to a server, not stored, and not shared with anyone. When you close the page, they are gone.

No accounts, no forms

There are no accounts, sign-ups or forms on SarkariRadar, and nothing to log in to. The site does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.

No data selling

Because the quiz does not collect personal information, there is nothing to sell. Your data is not sold or shared for advertising.
